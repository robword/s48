Judging Form

Use the questions as prompts and write notes for each section. Notes save automatically on this device only, not on the server. Use Print / Save PDF to hand in a copy.

Validation
  • Clear need identified?
  • Specific target market?
  • Did the team receive customer feedback on their product?
Execution & Design
  • Is there an MVP?
  • Is the MVP functional?
  • Is the product usable?
  • Clean & easy user interface?
  • Does the team work well together?
Business Model
  • Does the solution work?
  • Is there a value proposition?
  • Is the product unique?
  • Will they stand apart from their competition?
  • Do they have defined strategy to enter the market?
  • Do they have a sustainable funding model?
  • Is their user acquisition model realistic?
